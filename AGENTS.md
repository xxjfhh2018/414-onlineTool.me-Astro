# OnlineTool.me Project Instructions

When adding or materially rebuilding a calculator, read and follow [`docs/CALCULATOR_PAGE_STANDARD.md`](docs/CALCULATOR_PAGE_STANDARD.md) before implementation.

The calculator is not complete until its SEO content, calculation accuracy, interaction states, responsive layout, production build, and browser-based test cases all pass the checklist in that document.

Do not publish a time-sensitive calculator as current or official unless its source, applicable version or year, and update responsibility are documented. Label incomplete data models as demonstrations, or keep them unpublished.
